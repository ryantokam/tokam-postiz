Tokam Postiz privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Tokam Postiz is a self-hosted scheduler that only Ryan Tokam uses, for his own accounts. It can connect to YouTube (Google), Facebook, Instagram, Threads, X and TikTok.

What it accesses

When an account is connected, Tokam Postiz receives that account's name, username, profile picture and, where the platform provides it, email address. It also receives permission to publish posts and videos to that account and to read the account's posts and performance reports.

How it is used

This access is used only to publish and schedule posts the account owner chooses, and to show that account's performance to the account owner.

Where it is stored

Sign-in tokens and post data are stored in a database on Ryan Tokam's own computer. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Tokam Postiz's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Its use of each platform follows that platform's own terms, including the YouTube Terms of Service, the Google Privacy Policy, the Meta Platform Terms, the X Developer Agreement and the TikTok Developer Terms.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's settings, for example myaccount.google.com/permissions or Facebook's Apps and Websites settings. Deleting a channel in Tokam Postiz removes its stored tokens. To ask for any stored data to be deleted, email the address below; it is deleted within 30 days.
