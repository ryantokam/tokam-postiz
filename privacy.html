Tokam Postiz privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Tokam Postiz is a self-hosted scheduler that only Ryan Tokam uses, for his own accounts.

What it accesses

When an account is connected through Google sign-in, Tokam Postiz receives the account's name, email address and profile picture, and access to that account's YouTube channel: uploading and managing videos, reading channel details, and reading YouTube Analytics reports.

How it is used

This access is used only to publish and schedule videos the account owner chooses, and to show that channel's performance to the account owner.

Where it is stored

Sign-in tokens and post data are stored in a database on Ryan Tokam's own computer. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Tokam Postiz's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Its use of YouTube follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the channel in Tokam Postiz removes its stored tokens.
